# Continuous archive operations

- Each channel writer stream-copies ~20-second TS chunks. `current.m3u8` is a **12-entry sliding publisher manifest**, not playback history; the SQLite chunk index and on-disk chunks are durable. The 2-second poll reads only the sliding manifest. A cursor is committed atomically with chunk publication; startup and any poll that has missed the entire manifest window probe older finalized TS files omitted by rollover. This exceptional recovery may take time proportional to the unindexed files; normal polling does not scan the directory. The newest unlisted segment and `.tmp` files are not trusted.
- Indexed wall time comes from the completed chunk's filesystem modification time and media duration, **not FFmpeg's source `PROGRAM-DATE-TIME`**. Source stalls leave wall-clock gaps (and a new discontinuity epoch) rather than claiming continuous coverage. This is an approximation: clock skew, delayed file writes or sources that continue emitting data after a stall may affect the placement of a chunk; keep the server clock synchronized and verify physical-device seeking.
- Startup checks indexed files before reopening captures. Missing unreferenced rows lose their false coverage; referenced rows are quarantined in SQLite so saved-show and snapshot references remain auditable, but cannot be served as playable media. Missing files are logged. Do not remove a saved recording merely to silence that warning; restore its media or repair it deliberately.
- A finite playback ticket covers **at most 24 hours** and remains signed for **26 hours**, allowing a full-day watch without client renewal. Active snapshots pin their referenced chunks for that whole period. Retention cannot reclaim pins, so plan disk for the configured archive window **plus up to 26 hours of concurrently pinned windows per viewer/session**, subject to overlap. At 5 Mbps, one fully distinct 24-hour media window pinned for 26 hours is approximately 50.3 GiB. The 20 GiB free-space reserve and default 400 GiB archive quota can pause capture if outstanding pins consume headroom. Do not treat the quota as a guarantee of uninterrupted recording under many concurrent long-lived tickets. Tickets expire after 26 hours even if a player remains open; a later client renewal flow can reduce pin duration.
- All enabled current and future archives automatically attempt bounded, validated pair repair with raw-master fallback. No channel list or canary setting is needed. A clean, recently publishing source EOF gets a bounded fast capture retry on every channel; stalls, storage pressure and retry-budget exhaustion retain their longer backoffs. Unsupported or unproven seams stay raw; masters and existing snapshot pins never change. Derived copies consume the shared quota and filesystem reserve. For an emergency global raw-only rollback, set `STREAMVAULT_ARCHIVE_RAW_ONLY=1`, restart the server and open a new ticket. This does not repair upstream live response closures or prove physical-device playback and seeking.
